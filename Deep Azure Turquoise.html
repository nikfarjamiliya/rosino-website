Deep Azure Turquoise

Deep Azure Turquoise features a saturated blue tone broken by natural dark matrix patterns that add strong depth and contrast. The polished rectangular cut gives it a clean, modern look while keeping the raw character of the stone visible. Ideal as a statement in contemporary jewelry or as a stand-alone collectible for turquoise lovers.
