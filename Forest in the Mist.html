Forest in the Mist

Forest in the Mist is a moss agate that looks like a waking forest inside stone. Deep green growths rise like tall pines into a soft veil of natural mist at the top. Nothing is painted or arranged – the scene formed slowly on its own over countless years. A small, quiet landscape you can hold, full of depth, texture and stillness.
