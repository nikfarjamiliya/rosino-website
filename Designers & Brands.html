Why Rosino

We specialise exclusively in Landscape Agate, Persian Turquoise and Demantoid Garnet — stones selected for their natural character, rarity and visual depth.

  • Curated selections, not mass inventory. Every stone is hand-chosen to match the designer’s brief and the artistic direction of the collection.
  • Naturally rare materials. We work only with genuine, untreated stones sourced from small, consistent lots.
  • Stable and dependable supply to support long-term design and production schedules.
  • Direct sourcing with atelier-level quality, ensuring uniformity, clarity and precise finishing.
  • Tailored proposals for each brand, prepared around your concept and design language.
