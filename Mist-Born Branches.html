Mist-Born Branches

Mist-Born Branches is an agate built around pale, cream-coloured dendrites that lift gently through a fine mist. The branches seem almost weightless, like early spring growth emerging from fog. Each cluster formed slowly underground, giving the stone a warm, natural character with details that feel both delicate and clearly defined. A subtle, branch-like landscape for someone who prefers quiet pieces with structure. size 33.7mm
