Elysian Landscape Agate

Certificate available on request

Elysian Landscape Agate is a rare dendritic slice that holds a miniature world inside clear chalcedony. Fiery golds, soft greens and branching patterns form an almost painted landscape with striking depth. A single, one-of-a-kind piece for collectors, display, or a statement custom jewel. Approx. size: 57 mm.
