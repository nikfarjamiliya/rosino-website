Snowrise Dendritic Agate

Snowrise Dendritic Agate looks like a quiet winter morning captured in stone. Fine, branch-like dendrites rise through a soft, smoky background, almost like frost on glass or bare trees in pale light. When you move the cabochon, the patterns shift gently with the reflections, giving it a calm, focused presence. Nothing here is painted or added – it’s the slow work of minerals over millions of years. This piece works beautifully as the main stone in a unique jewel or as a small collectible you keep close when you want a moment of stillness. Size 20.3mm
