Embercrest Dendritic Agate

Certificate available on request

Embercrest Dendritic Agate is a dendritic agate cabochon with rich golden, amber and deep forest-green inclusions that form a vivid, tree-like landscape inside clear chalcedony. The polished oval cut enhances the contrast and depth, so the colours appear almost painted under the surface. A one-of-a-kind piece for serious collectors or refined custom jewelry. Approx. size: 38.8 × 26.1 mm.
